| R10 | 2026-06-10 | P2E | The current `uts_vlc` normalized outputs are document-level text blobs with `document_number`, `article_number`, and `clause_number` absent, so downstream chunking or citation work can silently over-assume article-level structure. | CONFIRMED | Require a dedicated article/chunk parsing phase before any retrieval, citation, or evaluation use. | R12 | 2026-06-10 | P2F | The Phase 2F parser produces `490` duplicate article hashes and `87` suspicious-length candidates, so naive downstream use would over-count repeated content and retain malformed chunks. | CONFIRMED | Add explicit Phase 2G filtering rules before any later retrieval or evaluation preparation. Keep parser outputs labeled as candidates only. | OPEN |
| R13 | 2026-06-10 | P2F | `299` parsed UTS documents contain non-empty preamble text before the first article marker, which means chunk boundaries can still be structurally incomplete even when article numbers parse correctly. | CONFIRMED | Preserve parse warnings, keep preamble handling explicit, and require filtering/review rules for preamble-bearing documents. | OPEN |
| R14 | 2026-06-10 | P2G | Phase 2G leaves `3,249` candidates in review-only status, so the dataset remains far from a clean downstream corpus and requires explicit human inspection. | CONFIRMED | Treat Phase 2G output as a review pack, not as a ready corpus. Do not promote to RAG or legal ground truth. | OPEN |
| R15 | 2026-06-10 | P2G | Duplicate hashes still exist in the Phase 2F/2G pipeline, and careless downstream selection could double-count repeated legal segments. | CONFIRMED | Keep duplicate-group reporting and canonical-choice logic visible in any future review workflow. | OPEN |
| R16 | 2026-06-10 | P2H | Manual-review decisions can vary across reviewers, which can make later corpus-candidate gates inconsistent even when the same sample pack is used. | CONFIRMED | Require a written rubric, reviewer template, and later adjudication rules before any downstream approval gate. | OPEN |
| R17 | 2026-06-10 | P2H | Stratified sampling still introduces sampling bias because the pack is intentionally review-focused and does not prove corpus-wide quality. | CONFIRMED | Keep the sample deterministic, report bucket coverage and shortfalls, and treat results as inspection evidence only. | OPEN |
| R18 | 2026-06-10 | P2H | Human reviewers may accidentally interpret parser candidates as legal meaning instead of structural review targets. | CONFIRMED | State explicitly that reviewers are not providing legal advice and that legal-expert review is a separate label. | OPEN |
| R19 | 2026-06-10 | P2H | Candidate text in the review pack can look authoritative even though it remains parser/filter output and not verified legal ground truth. | CONFIRMED | Keep `is_legal_ground_truth=false`, keep `approved_for_rag_index=false`, and block Phase 3. | OPEN |
| R20 | 2026-06-10 | P2I | Human review imports can silently drift if reviewers use free-form labels, non-ISO dates, or unknown confidence values. | CONFIRMED | Validate required fields centrally, reject unknown labels and confidence values, and require ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates before any later audit step. | OPEN |
| R21 | 2026-06-10 | P2I | A reviewer may accidentally set `legal_ground_truth_approved=true` or `rag_index_approved=true`, which would create a false downstream readiness signal. | CONFIRMED | Fail closed in the Phase 2I validator and require both approval flags to remain `false` for every row. | OPEN |

| R23 | 2026-06-10 | P2J | Duplicate or conflicting human review rows can make downstream corpus gates look stronger than they are if they are summarized without explicit conflict tracking. | CONFIRMED | Track duplicate candidate reviews, conflict counts, unresolved outcomes, and low-confidence rate in the Phase 2J audit report. | OPEN |
